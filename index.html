What does "concert" pitch mean?
Concert pitch is the note as it actually sounds — what a piano or tuner plays. On a transposing instrument the note you finger is named differently. Tap the concert note the conductor called out, and this shows the note to play so it sounds right.
—
How do I count the key signature?
Look at the start of the staff: count the sharps (♯) or flats (♭) next to the clef. Use − for flats and + for sharps. You don't need to know the key's name — the count is all this needs.
